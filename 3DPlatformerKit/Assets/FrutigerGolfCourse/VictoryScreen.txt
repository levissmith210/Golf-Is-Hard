GOLF IS HARD - TROPHY CELEBRATION

The trophy screen appears after completing every hole in the course. It uses the total round score, not just the last hole.

For the current nine-hole course:
- Diamond: at least 6 strokes under total par.
- Gold: total par or better.
- Silver: up to 9 strokes over total par.
- Bronze: finish the course.
Bubble collections are celebrated separately and never subtract golf strokes.

The sequence includes an animated 3D trophy, confetti, score count-up, personal-best flourish, scorecard summary, replay, and Main Menu.
Original synthesized sounds and a looping victory song work without any assigned audio files.

OPTIONAL AUDIO OVERRIDES
Select AeroGameplayRig > GolfCourseManagerObject and the Golf Victory Audio component.
Assign Victory Music, Course Fanfare, individual trophy reveal sounds, New Record Sound, and button sounds if desired.
An existing GolfCourseManager Course Complete Sound is used as a fallback fanfare, so it is played once.
Music/SFX/master settings and pause ducking apply to the celebration.

EDITOR PREVIEW
Tools > Golf is Hard > Preview Trophy Celebration
Preview all four awards, replay the reveal, and toggle the scorecard. This does not enter Play mode or save any score records.

Runtime files: GolfVictoryScreen, GolfVictoryTrophy, GolfVictoryAudio.
GolfScorecard opens the presentation on a completed round. Older scenes get the components automatically through Configure.
